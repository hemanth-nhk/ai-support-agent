CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY & OPERATIONS GUIDE

SECTION 1: RETURNS AND EXCHANGES
Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days of delivery for any eligible item. According to the uploaded document, the return period is 30 calendar days from the date of delivery. To initiate an exchange or return, please provide your original order number and receipt. All returned merchandise must remain in its original factory packaging, undamaged, and with all accessories included. Return shipping labels will be provided free of charge for damaged, defective, or incorrect shipments.

SECTION 2: SHIPPING AND EXPRESS DELIVERY
Orders are processed Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays. Standard ground shipping takes 3 to 5 business days nationwide. Express delivery options take 1 to 2 business days to arrive depending on regional transit hubs and fulfillment warehouse routing. Expedited shipping fees are non-refundable unless the shipment was delayed due to a fulfillment exception.

SECTION 3: REFUND TIMELINES AND WARRANTY COVERAGE
Once returned merchandise is received and inspected at our logistics facility, approved refunds are processed within 5 business days back to the original method of payment. Electronic products and hardware accessories are covered under a comprehensive 12-month manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and craftsmanship. Accidental damage or unauthorized modifications are not covered under the limited hardware warranty policy.
