Refund Policy

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive a returned item.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout.
Processing times after that can vary depending on your bank or card issuer, typically an additional 3-5 business days.
Partial refunds may be issued if an item is returned used or without original packaging.
